Can a 7B Model Beat a 70B Model?
Yes. On specific tasks, absolutely.
The formula has three ingredients:
1. Data Quality > Data Quantity
A 7B trained on curated, task-specific synthetic data outperforms a 70B trained on web-scale noisy data for domain tasks. The Cookbook shows how to build quality filtering pipelines that outperform scaling.
2. Training Recipe
SFT → GRPO/RLVR → On-Policy Distillation. This three-stage pipeline consistently produces the best small models. Each stage is a separate recipe in the Cookbook.
3. Evaluation
Smart evaluation is the secret weapon. Task-specific evals catch regressions that general benchmarks miss. Recipe #48 covers building evaluation pipelines.
The Cookbook includes a decision matrix: for any task, compute budget, and quality requirement, which training recipe to use.
