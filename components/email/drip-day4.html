The Death of RLHF?
PPO was the standard for RLHF for years. But 2025-2026 has been a Cambrian explosion of alternatives. Here's the landscape:
PPO Needs a critic. High variance. Stable but expensive.
GRPO No critic. Group-based advantage. Memory-efficient.
DAPO Dynamic sampling. Clip-free. Best for exploration.
RLVR Verifiable reward. Rule-based. Used in DeepSeek-R1.
The trend is clear: simpler algorithms, fewer components, less compute. PPO isn't dead, but the field is moving toward methods that work on consumer hardware.
