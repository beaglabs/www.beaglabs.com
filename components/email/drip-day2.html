The Smallest Models Are Getting Weirdly Good
Two years ago, a 7B model couldn't write working code. Today, DeepSeek-R1 distilled a 1.5B model that beats GPT-4 on math benchmarks.
The secret isn't the architecture — it's GRPO (Group Relative Policy Optimization). GRPO changed RL for LLMs by removing the critic network entirely. Instead of training a separate value model to judge outputs, it samples a group of responses and uses their relative scores as the signal.
This isn't a tutorial — it's the story of why small models suddenly matter. When you remove the critic, you halve the memory requirements. Training a 7B with GRPO fits on fewer GPUs. That changes who can play.
