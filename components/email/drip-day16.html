The Rise of Tiny Frontier Models
2026 is the year small models stopped being a compromise.
Phi-4 (Microsoft). 14B. Beats Llama-3-70B on reasoning benchmarks. Trained with curriculum learning — starting from synthetic data structured by difficulty.
Gemma 3 (Google). 27B best-in-class for size. Uses distillation from Gemini, then RL on top. The recipe is increasingly standardized: distill → RL → deploy.
Qwen 2.5 (Alibaba). 7B to 72B. Open-weight, permissive license. The 7B variant with RLVR and on-policy distillation competes with models 10x its size.
Why Architecture Matters →
All three models use different architectures (decoder-only, MoE, hybrid). The Cookbook breaks down each architecture's training requirements in Part 01.
The gap between "frontier" and "affordable" is collapsing. The Cookbook shows you how to train models in the 1B-27B range that used to require hundreds of GPUs.
