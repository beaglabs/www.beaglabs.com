Day 9
Reasoning Isn't Magic
The biggest shift in LLM training over the past year: teaching models to reason, not just respond.
The mechanism is surprisingly straightforward:
Outcome Supervision → Old Way
Reward is assigned at the final answer. The model learns to get the right answer by any means necessary — including shortcuts, cheats, and memorization. Works fine for simple tasks. Fails on multi-step reasoning.
Process Supervision → New Way
Reward is given at each reasoning step. The model learns to follow valid reasoning chains, not just reach correct answers. This is what powers models like o1, R1, and Gemini Thinking. The cost is human-labeled step-by-step rewards — or synthetic PRMs.
DeepSeek-R1 showed you can bootstrap process supervision without human labels — just use the model's own chain-of-thought as training signal. The result is a model that visibly "reasons" before answering.
