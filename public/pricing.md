# Pricing — Beag Labs

Beag Labs offers two engagement models for building and deploying domain-specific small language models. All custom models are exported as ONNX and deployed on your infrastructure — you own the weights.

## Done-For-You (Custom Model Build)

Beag Labs designs the labeling schema, runs the intelligent labeling pipeline, co-reviews edge cases with your team, trains the model, and ships a deployable ONNX export.

- **Price:** Custom — engagement-based. Contact [Book a call](https://cal.com/comradelemoncake/meet-the-founder)
- **Timeline:** Raw data to deployed model typically under 24 hours after schema alignment
- **What's included:**
  - Labeling schema design and frontier model selection
  - Intelligent auto-labeling via frontier models
  - Disagreement engine surfacing 2-5% of edge cases for human review
  - Keyboard-driven co-review interface for your domain experts
  - Model fine-tuning (500M-5B parameter range)
  - ONNX export with deployment documentation
  - You own the trained model weights — no runtime API dependency

## Platform (Train It Yourself)

Use the Beag Labs platform to run the training pipeline yourself with your own data.

- **Price:** Custom — contact us
- **What's included:**
  - Full access to the data connector, labeling, training, and export pipeline
  - OAuth data connectors (Gmail, GitHub, HubSpot, Notion) or CSV upload
  - Frontier model auto-labeling
  - Disagreement engine for edge-case surfacing
  - ONNX export
