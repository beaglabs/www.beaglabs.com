Solution areas

Four ways we put
agents to work.

Each area starts from systems you already own. We connect them, build practical ways for your team to use them, and keep people in charge of the decisions that matter.

01 / Documents → structured work

Document automation

Turn PDFs, forms and office files into structured information, reviewable actions and repeatable workflows.

How it runs
  • Read source documents
  • Extract and normalize fields
  • Review evidence and approve
Typical work

Grant files, permits, claims, invoices, contracts

02 / Requirements → working apps

Internal applications

Create the apps, forms and dashboards for processes your organization already runs, generated from a description and refined with your team.

How it runs
  • Describe the process
  • Generate usable interfaces
  • Validate, deploy, iterate
Typical work

Intake portals, trackers, review queues, reporting

03 / Legacy systems → modern services

Legacy modernization

Make terminals and SOAP/XML services usable through modern APIs and browser interfaces, while systems of record stay in place.

How it runs
  • Connect existing systems
  • Expose governed APIs
  • Give operators a better UX
Typical work

Mainframe screens, 3270 terminals, SOAP services

04 / Processes → governed automation

Back-office operations

Automate work that spans finance, procurement and operations with Papyrus: a data model, durable agents and human approvals.

How it runs
  • Model the records
  • Automate the routine path
  • Route exceptions to people
Typical work

Payables, receivables, reconciliation, case management
